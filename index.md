# Hyundai OBD2 Diagnostics - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Hyundai OBD2 Diagnostics does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

**Vehicle data.** Vehicle and diagnostic data received from a compatible ELM327 adapter is processed locally on the user's device. The app does not upload this diagnostic data to a developer-operated server.

**Permissions.** The app may request Bluetooth, nearby devices, location, Wi-Fi, and internet permissions only to discover and connect to compatible OBD2 adapters, communicate with the vehicle, and use Google Play services. Android may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some OS versions. The app does not use this permission to track or store the user's location.

**Advertising.** The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, and app interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where required, the app asks for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. Pro users do not see ads.

**Purchases.** Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google and is not received or stored by the developer.

**Voice alerts.** Spoken alerts use the text-to-speech engine installed on the device. No audio is recorded.

**Feedback and exports.** Reports, logs, or feedback messages are shared only when the user explicitly chooses an export, share, or send-feedback action, and only with the recipient the user selects. Data stored locally by the app can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

**Scope.** This app is intended for OBD2/EOBD-readable Hyundai vehicles. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hyundai.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Türkçe Özet

Hyundai OBD2 Diagnostics geliştiriciye kişisel veri göndermez, satmaz veya paylaşmaz. Araç ve arıza verileri kullanıcının cihazında yerel olarak işlenir. Bluetooth, yakın cihazlar, konum, Wi-Fi ve internet izinleri yalnızca uyumlu OBD2 adaptörüyle bağlantı kurmak ve Google Play hizmetlerini kullanmak içindir; konum izlenmez veya saklanmaz. Ücretsiz sürümde Google AdMob reklamları gösterilir; AdMob reklam kimliği gibi cihaz tanımlayıcılarını Google'ın gizlilik politikasına göre kullanabilir. Satın alımlar Google Play üzerinden yapılır. Raporlar ve geri bildirimler yalnızca kullanıcı açıkça paylaşmayı seçtiğinde gönderilir.
